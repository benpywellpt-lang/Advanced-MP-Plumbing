Got questions?

We've got answers

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No. There is no call-out charge and estimates are free. You'll know what the job costs before we start it.

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes, our registration number is 77903. Anyone working on gas in your home must by law be on the Gas Safe Register, and you can check any engineer at gassaferegister.co.uk.

Is your work guaranteed?

All our work is covered by a 1 year guarantee on parts and labour.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Hornchurch and have engineers available throughout Essex. If you're not sure whether we come out to you, call and ask.

Do you offer discounts for pensioners?

Yes, we offer OAP discounts. Mention it when you call or in the form and we'll include it in your estimate.

What is a power flush, and do I need one?

Over the years, sludge builds up inside a heating system. Radiators that are cold at the bottom, a noisy boiler and rooms that take a long time to warm up are the usual signs. A power flush pushes cleaning water through the system at speed to clear it out. We can tell you whether it's worth doing when we take a look.
