Privacy policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

Who we are

Advanced MP Plumbing & Heating Ltd is a plumbing and heating company based in Hornchurch, Essex, registered in England and Wales, company number 08693541. We are the data controller for the personal information described on this page.

If you have any questions about how we use your information, email pat@adv-mpplumbing.co.uk or call 01708 475 041.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to answer your enquiry and do the work you ask for.

  • Estimate form: your name, phone number, postcode, your email address if you choose to give it, what you need (a repair, heating work, a bathroom, a landlord certificate or a power flush) and anything you type about the job. If we ask you for photos and you send them, we keep those with your enquiry.
  • Phone and email: your contact details and the details of the job we discuss.
  • Doing the work: your address, access details, notes and photos from the visit, and the make and model of any boiler or equipment we fit, repair or service.
  • Billing: the details needed to raise an invoice and record payment.

We do not collect card details through this website and we do not run advertising or tracking cookies on it.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you an estimate.
  • To carry out the plumbing or heating work you ask for and keep a record of it, including gas safety records.
  • To invoice you and keep the accounting records we are required to hold.
  • To contact you about work we have done, for example a guarantee query, a landlord certificate renewal or a safety notice.

In data protection terms, we rely on taking steps at your request before entering a contract, performing that contract, complying with our legal obligations, and our legitimate interest in running the business and standing behind the work we guarantee. We do not use your details for marketing unless you have asked us to.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only where we need to:

  • Netlify hosts this website and processes estimate-form submissions before they reach us.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on this site, which means your browser requests them from Google when the page loads.
  • Suppliers and manufacturers, where a warranty registration or a parts order needs your name, address or the equipment details.
  • Our accountant and software providers, for invoicing and bookkeeping.
  • Regulators or the courts, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not go ahead: up to 12 months, so we can pick the conversation back up if you come back to us.
  • Records of work we have carried out: for as long as the guarantee runs plus a reasonable period afterwards. Gas safety records are kept for at least 2 years, as the regulations require.
  • Invoices and accounting records: 6 years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as required by HMRC.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, stop using it, or hand it over to you or another provider. You can also object to how we use it. Email pat@adv-mpplumbing.co.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies of its own and does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Your browser may store the site's fonts and images to make pages load faster; that is under your browser's control.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
